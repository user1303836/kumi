   Keys and commands                                                ↓ 67 more

   Send and steer
     enter               Send. While Kumi works, it reads your message after
                         the step under way
     tab                 While Kumi works: send it after the answer instead;
                         it waits above the box
     alt+↑               Take the last waiting message back into the box
     ctrl+j  alt+enter   A new line (shift+enter too, where the terminal
                         tells it apart)
     ↑ ↓                 The box's lines, then what you sent before
     ctrl+v              Add the picture on the clipboard to your next
                         message; files dragged in go with it too
     backspace           In an empty box, take back the last file added
